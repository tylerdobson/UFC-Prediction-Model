# Public UFC dataset survey

Checked **2026-09-26**. This survey responds to the request for more completed UFC events, bouts, fighters, and results. Counts below are publisher claims unless marked as a CSV line count. They are **not** deduplicated against our [research dataset](RESEARCH_DATASET_CARD.md), which now spans 1993–2026 with 790 events and 7,229 accepted bouts after a scoped identity review. A public download or repository license does not itself establish rights to its upstream fight data for a betting decision product.

### Additional identity and feed checks

- [Wikidata](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Reuse) releases its structured items under CC0 and gives fighters persistent QIDs plus a [UFC athlete ID property](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P9722). On September 26, 2026, a read-only Wikidata query found 1,880 items with that property; the no-English-Wikipedia-sitelink subset returned 144 property bindings across **143 distinct QIDs**. Exact-name comparison with the local held-identity review found **97 candidate fighters appearing 274 times across 268 distinct held bout positions**. This is an **identity-review lead**, not 268 importable bouts: each QID and UFC athlete slug must be checked against the exact event, opponent, and retained source revision before a scoped crosswalk is approved. The [worksheet runbook](WIKIDATA_IDENTITY_REVIEW.md) preserves the response and review context. The query is `?item wdt:P9722 ?ufcId` with `FILTER NOT EXISTS { ?article schema:about ?item ; schema:isPartOf <https://en.wikipedia.org/> . }` for the no-sitelink subset.
- [Dan McInerney's MMA AI artifact](https://huggingface.co/datasets/DanMcInerney/mma-ai) has a [release manifest](https://huggingface.co/datasets/DanMcInerney/mma-ai/resolve/main/manifest.json) claiming **7,704 fights, 4,587 fighters, and 764 events** through August 8, 2026, plus 1,362,886 timestamped BestFightOdds rows. Its [GitHub README](https://github.com/DanMcInerney/mma-ai) identifies UFCStats and BestFightOdds as upstream; Hugging Face marks the license “other.” Use this as a **QA and price-coverage reference only** until the underlying fight and odds rights are established. Its event endpoint also predates our September 2026 research import.
- [Cito's free UFC API](https://docs.citoapi.com/free-ufc-api/) advertises historical summary results and stable card IDs at 500 requests per month, with detailed fight statistics limited to the prior 30 days and no free odds. Its [terms](https://www.citoapi.com/terms/) explicitly say the aggregator is not a rights-cleared betting feed and direct wagering uses toward licensed data. It is an **API candidate for evaluation**, not an approved operating import.

## Decision and acceptance check

The Wikimedia result-history expansion is complete through the latest checked event on September 19, 2026. Use the Kaggle and GitHub files **as QA references only for now**, not as operating source rows or automatic identity mappings. Wikidata matches require bout-specific identity review. [UFC's published site terms](https://www.ufc.com/news/terms-use) restrict automated collection and commercial reuse of its site; whether and how those terms apply to a specific UFCStats-derived mirror needs confirmation. No publisher label above resolves that question for this betting-use project. See the [operating source decision](DATA_SOURCE_DECISION.md) for provider options and rights inquiries.

For any candidate later cleared for use, pin an immutable download/version and hash; validate unique event, fight, and fighter IDs; identify UFC-only completed fights; compare event dates, corners, outcomes, draws/no contests, and cancellations against independent evidence; resolve identity conflicts by review; and keep rejected rows visible. Build all pre-fight features from information available **before** each bout. Larger row counts do not make a training set trustworthy until these checks pass.
